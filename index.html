How to use FinTrack and answers to common questions.

How to Use FinTrack

FinTrack brings your bank and cash balances, expenses, liabilities, gold and SIP investments, friends splits and settlements into one secure dashboard. Use the navigation to open each area.

Financial Overview

Shows your total bank and cash balance, liabilities, savings and gold, and net financial balance. Use Visibility Settings to hide liabilities or savings for a simpler view.

Monthly Expenses

Log shared monthly expenses with categories. Each entry can be deducted from a chosen account.

Income

Use the Add Money feature to record income or deposits into any saved account.

Bank and Cash

Add your bank accounts and cash balances under the two configured bank account types.

Bank and Cash Transfers

Record transfers between your accounts. Balances update automatically.

Credit Cards

Track credit card outstanding amounts and due dates. Reminders appear near the due date.

Debt and Liabilities

Track loans with payment logs and closing status.

Gold and SIP

Record gold purchases and SIP investments with conversion analysis.

Savings Goals

Use SIP investments to track progress towards your savings goals.

Friends

Search registered FinTrack users from Profile > Friends and send connection requests for shared expenses.

Friends Split

Split expenses with friends. Registered friends sync between both accounts; non-registered friends are tracked manually.

Settlements

Record household, friend and shared settlements in one area. The history shows the settlement type for each record.

Profile

View your personal information, friends and notifications.

Settings

Configure visibility, currencies, exchange rates and people.

Is My Data Safe?

Your data is stored securely in Firebase and is only accessible with your account.

Who Can View My Data?

Only you can view your financial data. Shared records are visible only to the connected users involved.

Is My Password Stored in FinTrack?

No. Passwords are managed securely by Firebase Authentication and are never stored in the FinTrack database.

Can Another User Access My Account?

No. Each account is private. Only shared splits and settlements you create with another user are visible to that user.

What Happens to My Financial Data When the App Is Updated?

Updates never delete your data. Hiding a section only hides the display; your records remain intact.

Can I Use More Than One Currency?

Yes. Use Keep Selected Currency Only to limit the app to one or two currencies. INR remains the default base currency.

Why Do I See Categories When Adding an Expense?

Categories help you analyse spending patterns across Food, Transport, Shopping, Bills and Other.

What Is the Purpose of the Expense Graph?

The expense graph helps you understand spending patterns by category over time.
